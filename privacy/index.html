Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

MyClinicDesk is clinic management software for doctors and clinics in India. This policy explains what information MyClinicDesk handles, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. It applies to the website myclinicdesk.com and the MyClinicDesk app at api.myclinicdesk.com.

In short: clinics own the patient records they keep in MyClinicDesk. We store them only to run the service for that clinic, keep each clinic's records separate, never sell data, and never use it for advertising.

1. Who is responsible for what

Clinics and doctors who sign up decide which patient information to record and why. For patient records they are the data fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and are responsible for telling their patients and obtaining any consent that is needed.

MyClinicDesk processes patient records on the clinic's behalf, only to provide the service. For clinic and doctor account details, and for visitors to our website, MyClinicDesk decides how the information is used, as described in this policy.

Patients who want to see, correct or remove their records should contact their clinic first. We will help the clinic respond.

2. Information we handle

Account and clinic details

  • Doctor and staff names, email addresses, usernames and passwords. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form.
  • Clinic profile: clinic name, address, phone, working hours, doctor's qualifications and council registration number, and the logo, letterhead, signature and stamp images a clinic chooses to upload.
  • Clinic settings such as the registration form, medicine list, price list and note boxes.

Patient records entered by a clinic

  • Patient details such as name, age, sex, phone number, address and other fields on the clinic's registration form.
  • Medical history answers, visits, complaints, diagnoses, notes, treatment plans, prescriptions, certificates, appointments, invoices and payments.
  • Consent records and signatures captured at the clinic or on the patient registration form.
  • Reports (PDFs or photos) uploaded by the clinic or sent by the patient through an upload link from the clinic.

Technical information

  • A login cookie that keeps you signed in (see Cookies).
  • An activity log of important actions in each clinic account, such as sign-ins, imports, deletions and backups, with the time and the account that did them. It is used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and errors, kept for security and to fix problems.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell any information.

3. How we use information

  • To provide MyClinicDesk: storing and showing records, printing and sharing prescriptions, invoices, certificates and reports, appointments and reminders, analytics for the clinic, and backups.
  • To sign people in, keep accounts secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To send service emails such as password resets.
  • To answer support requests that you send us.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Patient records are used only to provide the service to the clinic that entered them. We do not look at them except when the clinic asks us to help, when needed to keep the service secure and working, or when required by law.

4. Google sign-in and Google Drive

Sign in with Google. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID, and use them only to identify your account.

Google Drive backup. A doctor can connect a Google account so that every night MyClinicDesk saves a backup (a zip of Patient Report PDFs and an Excel file of the clinic's records) to a "MyClinicDesk Backups" folder in that Google Drive. For this we:

  • request only the drive.file permission, so MyClinicDesk can see and manage only the files it creates itself, not any other file in your Drive;
  • store the Google account email and an access token, encrypted, so the nightly backup can run;
  • build each backup in memory, upload it straight to your Drive, and then erase it. Backup files are not kept on our servers;
  • keep the newest 30 backups in that folder and remove older backups that MyClinicDesk created.

You can disconnect at any time in Settings › Google Drive Backup. This deletes the stored token and revokes MyClinicDesk's access with Google. Backups already in your Drive stay there. You can also remove access from your Google Account's security settings.

MyClinicDesk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not let people read it except as described above.

5. WhatsApp and shared links

  • When a clinic sends a message on WhatsApp, MyClinicDesk opens WhatsApp on the clinic's own phone or computer with the text filled in. The clinic sends it from its own WhatsApp account, and we do not receive the conversation.
  • To send a prescription, invoice, certificate or report as a PDF from a computer, the PDF is stored on our servers and the message carries a private link. These links stop working after 3 days and the stored PDF is then deleted. On phones the PDF is shared directly and is not stored by us.
  • Report upload links that a clinic sends to a patient work for 7 days and for up to 10 files. Files sent through them go only to that clinic's account.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run MyClinicDesk, under their own security and confidentiality commitments:

  • Cloud hosting and database (Railway), where the app and its database run.
  • Google, for sign-in and, if a doctor connects it, Google Drive backup.
  • Email delivery, for password-reset emails.
  • GitHub Pages and Google Fonts, which serve this website and its fonts.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users or the public, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of the service, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

7. Where information is stored and for how long

Information is stored with our cloud hosting provider, whose servers may be located outside India. Each clinic's records are kept separate and are visible only to that clinic's signed-in users.

  • Clinic and patient records are kept for as long as the clinic's account is active, so the clinic can meet its own record-keeping duties. Clinics can download a full backup at any time.
  • If a clinic asks us to close its account, we delete its records within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer.
  • Deleting a patient in MyClinicDesk deletes that patient's visits, prescriptions, invoices, appointments, reports and shared links.
  • Shared PDF links are deleted after 3 days. Upload links expire after 7 days.

8. Security

  • All connections use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and Google Drive tokens are encrypted.
  • Every request is checked against the signed-in clinic, so one clinic cannot see another clinic's records.
  • Uploaded files are checked by their content and accepted only as PDF or image files.
  • Important actions are recorded in each clinic's activity log.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password, do not share accounts, and sign out on shared computers. If you think an account has been misused, contact us right away.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to ask for a summary of your personal data, to correct or update it, to have it erased, to withdraw consent, and to raise a grievance.

  • Patients: please contact your clinic, which controls your records. You can also write to us and we will pass your request to the clinic.
  • Doctors, staff and website visitors: write to myclinicdesk.support@gmail.com.

We will respond within 30 days.

10. Cookies

The MyClinicDesk app uses one essential cookie to keep you signed in, plus a short-lived cookie during Google sign-in to protect it from forgery. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app may also keep small preferences, such as whether you have seen a tip, in your browser's local storage.

11. Children

MyClinicDesk is for clinics, not for children to use. Clinics may record information about patients who are children, and they are responsible for obtaining any consent from a parent or guardian that the law requires.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will change the date at the top, and for important changes we will tell clinics in the app or by email.
