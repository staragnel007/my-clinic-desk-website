Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. MyClinicDesk runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Your data is available from any device after you sign in.

Can patients book appointments online?

Yes. You get a booking link and a QR code. Patients pick a doctor and slot, and the booking appears instantly in your queue.

How do WhatsApp reminders work?

Once enabled, patients automatically receive booking confirmations, queue updates, prescriptions, bills and follow-up reminders on WhatsApp.

Is there a limit on the number of patients?

No. Every plan includes unlimited patient records and visits.

Is my patients’ data safe?

What happens when my free trial ends?

You can choose a plan and continue with all your data intact. If you don’t upgrade, your account is paused — nothing is deleted.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with no penalty, and you can export your data before you leave.
